// The original assertion fails because the transition from S_WAIT to S_DONE depends on dmem_ptw_comm_i.resp.valid being asserted and the PTE being a leaf, but the next_state update occurs only after the clock edge. Therefore, the assertion should be a next-cycle assertion (|=>) and the precondition should use the wire dmem_ptw_comm_i.resp.valid directly, while the postcondition should check ptw.next_state without $past() because next_state is a register updated on the clock edge. Also, the signal prefix for dmem_ptw_comm_i.resp.valid must be correct (ptw.dmem_ptw_comm_i.resp.valid).
as__WaitStatePteLeafNextDone: assert property ((ptw.current_state == ptw.S_WAIT && ptw.dmem_ptw_comm_i.resp.valid && ptw.is_pte_leaf) |=> (ptw.next_state == ptw.S_DONE));
