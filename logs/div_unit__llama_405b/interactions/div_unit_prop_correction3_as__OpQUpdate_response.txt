Proposed fix:
The assertion should be modified to only check the update of op_q when flush_i is low and valid_i is high. This can be achieved by adding a condition to the precondition of the assertion.

Corrected assertion:
```
as__OpQUpdate: assert property(valid_i && !flush_i |=> div_unit.op_q == $past(op_i));
```
